Privacy Policy for Farming and Supermarket Simulator

Last updated: August 8, 2026

EY08 Games ("we", "our", or "us") operates the PC video game Farming and Supermarket Simulator. This Privacy Policy explains how data is captured, used, shared, and retained when you play our game via the Epic Games Store.

1. Data Capture (How Data Is Collected)

We collect information directly through your interactions with the game and via integrated Epic Games Store Online Services (EOS) APIs:

  • Account Identifiers: Your Epic Account ID (PUID) is automatically captured upon launcher authentication.
  • Gameplay Data: In-game progress, achievement unlocks, inventory states, and cloud save files.

2. Data Usage (How Data Is Used)

Captured data is utilized strictly for essential gameplay features, including:

  • Authenticating player access and maintaining persistent save files.
  • Synchronizing cloud save progression across devices.
  • Unlocking and tracking in-game achievements on the Epic Games Store.

3. Data Sharing (How Data Is Shared)

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data to third parties or advertisers. Data is shared exclusively with integrated service providers strictly necessary to deliver the game service:

  • Epic Games Store Services: Shared via Epic Online Services (EOS) SDK for player authentication and cloud features in accordance with the Epic Games Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention (How Long Data Is Kept)

We retain captured account identifiers and gameplay data only for as long as necessary to fulfill game functionality:

  • Active Accounts: Game progress and achievement data are retained for the duration of your active account usage.
  • Data Deletion Requests: Players can request complete deletion of their stored save data or identifiers at any time by contacting us at yildizveemre@gmail.com. Upon receiving a valid request, data will be permanently purged within 30 days.

5. Children's Privacy

Farming and Supermarket Simulator is a general-audience video game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. We strictly adhere to COPPA and GDPR regulations.
